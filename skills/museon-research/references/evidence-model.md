# Evidence model

## Mental model

The research question determines platform, source family, and horizon. Strong evidence is opened or visually inspected; named creators/posts retain source URLs. Creative claims require the visual object, not caption/transcript alone. The result should end in an operating decision: direction, reusable format, account action, brief, monitoring plan, or next test.

Content Analyzer creates a durable video analysis run from a supported platform URL, Museon media, or local video. Its completed `share_url` is the inspection surface.

## Shortcuts

| Evidence shape | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Source/page facts | `museoncli research +web-research` |
| Social/community observations | Matching research search shortcut |
| Visual claims | `museoncli research +visual-analyze` |
| Video structure | `museoncli content-analysis +run` |

## DON'T

- **DON'T** collect a long weak list instead of inspecting the strongest sources.
- **DON'T** merge observed fact, interpretation, inference, and confidence into one claim.
- **DON'T** publish a named creator/post conclusion without its source URL.
- **DON'T** omit item IDs supporting numeric claims from Creative Search Ads snapshots.

## Relationships

Assets preserve reusable creative structure; campaign-monitor preserves monitored history; artifacts preserve a shareable synthesis.
