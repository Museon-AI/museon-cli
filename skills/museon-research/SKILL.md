# Museon research

Use for the `research` and `content-analysis` domains. Discover the exact schema before use and
keep source links beside load-bearing claims.

| Command family | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `museoncli research +social-media-search` | Platform-native creators, posts, trends, and comments |
| `museoncli research +community-search` | X, Reddit, and LinkedIn discussions and sentiment |
| `museoncli research +web-research` | Public web pages, market facts, and official assets |
| `museoncli research +visual-analyze` | Images, screenshots, ads, and video frames |
| `museoncli content-analysis +run` | Async analysis of supported videos and media |
