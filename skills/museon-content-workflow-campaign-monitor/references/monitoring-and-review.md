# Monitoring and review

Use `campaign-monitor` for monitored creators, content, campaign summaries, and locally synced post histories. It is not a substitute for live public-platform search.

Choose the source matching the reviewed object:

- `social-account` for connected or operated account performance.
- `campaign-monitor` for monitored creators, content, campaign summaries, and locally synced post histories.
- `account-operation` for managed-fleet lifecycle, runs, attribution, and intervention status.

Compare what was planned, generated, and published with what actually worked. Distinguish performance facts from interpretation. Turn the review into a specific next action: reuse a format, adjust a topic, pause a weak element, change a schedule, collect more evidence, or run a new test.

Inspect `museoncli schema campaign-monitor`; commands implemented in `museoncli/domains/social_media.py` still use `Domain.CAMPAIGN_MONITOR` and belong here.
